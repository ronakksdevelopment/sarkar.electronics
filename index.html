About the shop

Run by one owner, known by name

What reviewers keep coming back to say about the place.

Sarkar Electronics is a locally run electronics store in Krishna Nagar, Agartala, operated by Anup Sarkar. The shop has been registered as a GST wholesaler and distributor since 2018, stocking a full range of electronic goods and consumer appliances for the home, from lighting and wiring to large appliances and audio.

Alongside the physical shop, the business runs an online storefront with individual product listings, and customers consistently mention fair pricing and Anup Sarkar's own attentive, courteous service. He is known to reply to reviews personally and stand behind what he sells.
