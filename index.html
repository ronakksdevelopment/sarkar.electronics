01 — About the shop

Run by one owner, known by name

What reviewers keep coming back to say about the place.

Sarkar Electronics is a locally run electronics store in Krishna Nagar, Agartala, operated by Anup Sarkar. The shop has been registered as a GST wholesaler/distributor since 2018, and stocks a general range of electronic goods and consumer appliances — LED bulbs are called out specifically on its wholesale listing.

Alongside the physical shop, the business runs an online storefront with individual product listing pages, and customers consistently mention fair pricing and Anup Sarkar's own attentive, courteous service — he's known to reply to reviews personally.
